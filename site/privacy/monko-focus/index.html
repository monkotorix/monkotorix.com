Privacy policy for Monko Focus

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Monko Focus is a set of calm, playful games for children aged 4 to 8, for phones and tablets. It is published by Sofmon B.V. in the Netherlands (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information in the app, and what reaches us. It is written for parents and other grown-ups.

In short

  • Monko Focus collects no personal information. The app sends nothing about your child to anyone, and nothing about you to us.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no chat and no links out of the app.
  • It does not connect to the internet itself. Only the store’s purchase software, for the full game, talks to the app store (see below). Everything else it needs, including the buddy’s voice, is inside the app.
  • The app is free to try. One in-app purchase, made by a grown-up through the app store, opens the full game. The store handles the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • What a grown-up sets up (such as the child’s first name and age) and the child’s progress stay in the app’s own storage on the device. Deleting the app deletes them from the device.

For children: Monko Focus does not send anything about you anywhere. Your name, your stars and your stickers stay on this tablet or phone.

What the app keeps on the device

So that the games remember where your child is and how you set them up, the app keeps the following in its own storage on the device:

  • What a grown-up enters, all of it optional: the child’s first name, age and gender, what helps your child (calm mode, a slower speed, extra breaks), the kinds of games to play most, favourite things for the stickers, the chosen buddy, and your own 4-digit PIN for the grown-up corner if you set one.
  • The settings: language, sound and voice, difficulty and the other choices in the grown-up corner.
  • Progress: stars, stickers and badges, the level each game has reached and when it was last played, where your child is on the magic path, and which game instructions your child has already heard.

This information is used only inside the app: to choose the levels, to show progress in the grown-up corner, and to show your child’s name in the game, for example on the celebration screen. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. The app’s storage is private to the app, so other apps cannot read it, and the device’s own security, such as its screen lock and encryption, protects it.

How to see, change or delete it

  • Everything above can be seen and changed in the grown-up corner: press and hold the gear next to ? on the map, then type the number you see.
  • Remove profile (in the grown-up corner, under Child & buddy) deletes the profile and the progress from the device and puts every setting back to how it started. Only the chosen language, and whether the full game is bought, stay.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it kept from the device. Some devices also let you clear the app’s storage in their settings, which does the same without deleting the app. Offloading the app to save space, where your device offers it, keeps the app’s data.

Backups made by your device

If your device is backed up, the backup may include the app’s data, as it can for any app:

  • your device’s own backup, for example to the cloud or to a computer;
  • copying everything to a new phone or tablet.

These backups are yours. They are kept under your account and the backup service’s own privacy policy, and we cannot see them. Restoring a backup brings the app’s data back, and a copy can stay in an older backup after you delete the app, until that backup is replaced or deleted. You can manage or switch off backups in your device’s settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party services that collect data, apart from the store’s own purchase software for the full game (see below). The rest of the app is built with open-source software (such as Capacitor and React) that runs inside the app and sends nothing anywhere.
  • No internet connection of its own. Only the store’s purchase software in the app connects, to the app store, for the full game (see below).
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. The app asks you for no permissions at all, and it never listens to or records your child. On some devices the app’s details list the system’s standard internet and network-state permissions and the store’s billing permission: these are never asked of you, and only the purchase uses them.
  • No chat, no sharing and no links out of the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children’s privacy

Monko Focus is made for children aged 4 to 8 and is meant to be set up by a grown-up. After the first setup, everything meant for grown-ups is behind a gate: press and hold, then type a number written in words (or your own PIN).

The app follows the app stores’ rules for apps made for children: no advertising, no third-party analytics, nothing about your child leaving the device and no links out of the app. The one purchase, the full game, is only offered behind the grown-up gate: a child sees a padlock and “Ask a grown-up”, never a price or a buy button.

Laws such as the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe protect children’s personal information, for example by requiring a parent’s consent before it is collected. Monko Focus collects none, so we do not ask for consent, and nothing from inside the app reaches us to show, change or delete. The most we may have is an email you sent us, the store’s record of a purchase or a review (see below). If you think we have received information about your child in some way, please contact us and we will delete it.

Buying the full game

The first world and four games are free, and one in-app purchase opens the rest. It is bought in the grown-up corner, behind the gate. The app store you got the app from handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The store’s purchase software in the app asks the store only whether the full game is bought for this account, or shared with it by the family. The app keeps that answer in its own storage, so the game also opens when the device is offline.

This purchase software talks to the store, and may send it technical information about the purchase and about how the software is working, under the store’s own privacy policy. The app gives it only the product’s ID, nothing about your child. There is no server of ours in between.

What the app stores may tell us

Each app store runs under its own privacy policy. It gives us reports of downloads and purchases, such as how many there were per country. Depending on the store, we also see a record of each purchase: the order number, date, product, price and country, but never payment details. If you ask us for help with a purchase or a refund, we may look up your order in the store’s tools, for example by the email address of your store account. We get crash reports only if you chose in your device’s settings to share them with app developers. None of this includes your child’s name or anything else the app keeps on the device. If you rate or review the app in a store, we see your review and the name the store shows with it.

We use this only to answer questions, to help with purchases and refunds, to fix problems in the app and to keep our books. Our basis for this under the GDPR is our legitimate interest in supporting the app, and for our books, our legal duty to keep them. We keep the records our books need as long as Dutch law requires: 7 years.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you. Our basis for this under the GDPR is our legitimate interest in answering questions about our app. We delete emails 12 months after the last message, unless they concern a purchase or a refund: those we keep as long as bookkeeping law requires.

Our email provider handles our email for us, under its data processing terms. It may process emails outside the EU, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found gives adequate protection.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights over the information we hold about you. You can ask us:

  • to tell you what information we hold about you, and to give you a copy;
  • to correct it or delete it;
  • to restrict how we use it, for example while a question about it is being settled.

You can also object to our use of it where it rests on our legitimate interest, such as answering your email or helping with a purchase.

Send your request to the email address under Contact. Nothing from inside the app reaches us: everything the app keeps stays on your device, and you control it as described above. What we may hold is an email you sent us, the store’s record of a purchase or a review. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands, where we are based (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

Changes to this policy

We do not plan to collect any information through the app. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before the new version of the app is released, change the date at the top, and ask for a parent’s consent first where the law requires it.
